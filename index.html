About The Centre

Advanced Neurosurgical Care with a Minimally Invasive Approach

At Naveen’s Neuro-Spine Centre, we take pride in our commitment to cutting-edge healthcare. As a leader in the field of neurosurgery, we specialize in minimal access neurosurgery—a pioneering approach that minimizes invasiveness while maximizing patient outcomes.

Minimal access neurosurgery, also known as minimally invasive neurosurgery, revolutionizes the treatment of brain and spine conditions. This innovative technique employs small incisions and specialized instrumentation to address complex neurological and spine issues with precision and care.

With minimal access neurosurgery, we offer reduced recovery times, minimal scarring, and improved patient comfort. We are proud to be at the forefront of this medical advancement, delivering hope and healing to those in need.

Pioneering Technique

Smaller Access. Greater Precision.

Minimally invasive neurosurgery (minimal access neurosurgery) utilizes advanced endoscopic cameras, tubular retractors, and micro-instruments to reach brain and spine conditions through keyhole openings.

By navigating natural anatomical pathways, this approach aims to minimize muscle tearing and tissue disruption, offering significant clinical advantages compared to traditional open surgery.

Patient Education

Brain Tumours – What Should You Know?

A brain tumour is an abnormal growth of cells in or around the brain. Brain tumours can be benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous), and their symptoms and treatment depend on their type, size and location.

Common Warning Symptoms to Watch For:

Persistent or unusual headaches
New-onset seizures or tremors
Repeated morning vomiting
Vision or speech changes
Weakness on one side of body
Balance or gait problems
Changes in memory, behavior, or personality
Important Clinical Note: These symptoms can have many causes and do not necessarily indicate a brain tumour. Persistent, severe or concerning symptoms should be medically evaluated by a specialist.
